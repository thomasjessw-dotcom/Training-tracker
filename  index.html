10-Minute Mobility

Use this on recovery days, before soccer, or anytime you feel stiff. Move smoothly — this should loosen you up, not fatigue you.

1. Ankle rocks — 10/side
Keep your heel down and drive your knee forward over your toes.
2. 90/90 hip switches — 10 total
Sit tall and rotate both knees side to side under control.
3. World’s greatest stretch — 5/side
Long lunge, elbow toward inside ankle, then rotate your chest upward.
4. Adductor rock-backs — 10/side
One knee down, opposite leg straight to the side. Rock your hips back.
5. Hamstring sweeps — 10/side
Step one heel forward and sweep your hands toward your toes.
6. Deep squat hold + shifts — 30–45 sec
Sit into a comfortable squat and gently shift side to side.
7. Thoracic rotations — 8/side
Rotate through your upper back while keeping your hips mostly still.
